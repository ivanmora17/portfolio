    Second prize at Marbella Startup Lab

    Together with my team I built and pitched Cobijo at the 2nd edition of Marbella Startup Lab, the entrepreneurship program run by Marbellup and Marbella City Council, and won second place and a €500 prize.

    Cobijo is a long-term rental platform built around one idea: owners with empty flats don't rent them out for fear of unpaid rent, and tenants with jobs can't find a home because of the guarantees landlords demand. Owners get rent protection, legal defence and eviction handling for one month's rent a year; tenants move in with just the legal deposit and are shortlisted without their name, age, nationality or photo being shown. We presented it with a working prototype of the app.
